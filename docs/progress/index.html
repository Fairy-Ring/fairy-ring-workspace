Skills and minigames

Pre-Slayer skills ship. Slayer assign + kill is in. Farming is Falador potato only — do not invent disease rates. Construction is out of era.

Castle Wars, Duel Arena, Trawler, Treasure Trails, and similar inherited minigames are present and unproven in multiplayer. Pest Control boards the waiting lander (well, not the plank); there is still no match instance. Barrows Ahrim Dig, six Climb-up dests, and Ahrim sarcophagus Search ship; the kill-count overlay opens as a dummy. Champions’ Challenge has Imp through Lesser Demon residual fights. The victory parchment and Fight Pit HUD stay closed. Fight Pits and Fight Caves first clicks are honest no-ops. Leon is later.

Residual interfaces now open: Temple Trekking route map, Soul’s Bane rage bar (A Soul’s Bane itself still has no quest start), and cat naming after Felkrash. Twenty-one named residual interfaces still have no script.

A Fairy Tale Part II, Royal Trouble, Trouble Brewing, Construction / player-owned houses. Those are after ~2 May 2006.
